All replay numbers are historical replay evidence, not a guarantee about future markets.

The v2 held-out Brier score was slightly worse after correction: `0.1984` versus raw `0.1955` (bootstrap 95% intervals are in `results/latest-replay-v2.json`). Calibration error improved from `0.1453` to `0.1022`. No accuracy-improvement claim is made. The nine current live calls are real records, not placeholders; each market ID, venue URL, Tatum price endpoint, snapshot timestamp, raw price, Walrus blob, and Sui transaction digest is recorded in `results/live-calls.json`. The live records have empty `citedBlobIds`; the UI therefore says that each call's bucket in Walrus Memory holds the recorded number of past cases, rather than claiming a memory was cited at decision time. `VO-LIVE-01` and `VO-LIVE-03` were committed with Tatum `rawPrice: 0` and are shown as `Price unavailable`. The original six are retained as the first sample in `results/live-calls-first-sample.json`.
